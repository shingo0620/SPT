# Wikipedia Featured — 2026-09-15

## 今日精選文章：Menches

Menches was an ancient Egyptian official who served as the village scribe of Kerkeosiris from before 119 BC until 110 BC. He lived during the Ptolemaic period, when a Greek dynasty ruled Egypt. He was also known by the Greek name Asclepiades and may have been descended from Greek settlers, although little is known of his personal life.
