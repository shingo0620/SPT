# Wikipedia Featured — 2026-09-11

## 今日精選文章：American Airlines Flight 11

American Airlines Flight 11 was a scheduled domestic transcontinental passenger flight from Boston to Los Angeles. On the morning of September 11, 2001, it was hijacked by Mohamed Atta and four other al-Qaeda hijackers, one of four aircraft hijacked as part of the September 11 attacks. Atta deliberately flew the aircraft into the North Tower of the World Trade Center complex in New York City, killing all 92 people aboard, including the five hijackers. The impact destroyed all escape routes from the floors above the impact zone, trapping more than 1,300 occupants on floors 92–110, none of whom survived. People below the impact zone were also killed as a result of the attack.
