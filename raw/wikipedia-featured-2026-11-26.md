## 今日精選文章：Tabancale language

Tabancale is an extinct language formerly spoken in the village of Aconipa, located along the Chinchipe River in the Department of Cajamarca, Peru, as well as in neighboring Ecuador, by the Tabancale people. Only five words were collected in the 16th century and they are not demonstrably related to those in other languages. As a result, it has been categorized as a language isolate by some and left unclassified by others; a connection has been proposed with the Chicham languages, spoken in Peru and Ecuador. The Tabancale people are thought to have switched to Quechua after they were conquered in 1549.
