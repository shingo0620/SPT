## 今日精選文章：Islands: Non-Places

Islands: Non-Places is a 2016 abstract art game developed and published independently by artist and animator Carl Burton, who is best known for the animated GIF illustrations he created for season two of the Serial podcast. The game was released on November 17, 2016, on iOS, Windows, Mac, and Linux, and consists of a series of anonymous, liminal environments – the eponymous "non-places". These scenes appear mundane at first, but become increasingly surreal as the player interacts with the objects within.
