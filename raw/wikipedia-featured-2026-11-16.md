## 今日精選文章：Mark Satin

Mark Ivor Satin is an American political theorist, writer, and newsletter publisher. He contributed to the development and dissemination of three political perspectives – neopacifism in the 1960s, New Age politics in the 1970s and 1980s, and radical centrism from the 1990s into the 2020s. Satin's work is sometimes seen as building toward a new political ideology, and then it is often labeled "transformational", "post-liberal", or "post-Marxist". One historian calls Satin's writing "post-hip".
