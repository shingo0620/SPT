## 今日精選文章：Pat O'Keeffe

Pat O'Keeffe was a British professional boxer who twice held the British middleweight title. His professional career spanned sixteen years, from 1902 to 1918, and took him to the United States and Australia between 1907 and 1910. In 1914 he challenged Georges Carpentier in a contest billed as the European heavyweight championship and was knocked out in two rounds.
